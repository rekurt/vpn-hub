# Code of Conduct

VPN Hub is maintained for operators working with sensitive network infrastructure. Be respectful, specific, and professional in issues, reviews, discussions, and private security reports.

Do not publish personal data, credentials, host identifiers, network topology, provider links, or unredacted logs. Critique designs and changes, never people. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or deliberate disclosure of sensitive information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other action needed to protect contributors and users.
